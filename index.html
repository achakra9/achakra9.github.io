About

Welcome! I am Arnab Chakraborty, a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Southern California. My research focuses on developing advanced methodologies in electronic-structure theory to address challenging problems in molecular science, and on building robust software implementations for production quantum chemistry codebases.

Let’s connect

When I’m not immersed in research or coding, I enjoy exploring machine learning, designing scientific graphics with open-source tools, and learning from open-source projects. Feel free to reach out to collaborate or chat.

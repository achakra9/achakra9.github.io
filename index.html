Selected research

All research & software ↗

MRSF-TDDFT

Implementation of mixed-reference spin-flip time-dependent density functional theory (MRSF-TDDFT) in Q-Chem for energies, transition dipole moments, oscillator strengths, spin–orbit couplings, and density-matrix-based analyses of electronic states and transitions. Benchmarks include molecules with variable diradical character, butadiene, and conical intersections in a reduced retinal model (PSB3).

J. Chem. Phys. ChemRxiv

Semi-stochastic CC(P;Q)

Semi-stochastic CC(P;Q) methods combining deterministic CC/EOM-CC ideas with CIQMC propagations to approach high-level excited-state energetics efficiently.

Mol. Phys. J. Chem. Phys.

H3+ formation from methyl halogens & pseudohalogens

Combined experiments, CC/EOM-CC calculations, and ab initio molecular dynamics to understand roaming-driven H3+ formation and its trends across CH3X species.

Nat. Commun.

I also explore machine learning for chemistry, scientific graphics, and open-source tools.

Current appointment and research focus.

Postdoctoral Research Associate

May 2024 — Present

University of Southern California

U.S.A

Advisor: Professor Anna I. Krylov
Focus: Development of mixed-reference spin-flip TDDFT (MRSF-TDDFT) in Q-Chem for excited-state energies, properties, and density-matrix-based analyses.
  • Developed and integrated Mixed-Reference Spin-Flip TDDFT (MRSF-TDDFT) into the Q-Chem electronic-structure package (released as part of Q-Chem 6.4 in December 2025), providing excitation energies, oscillator strengths, and transition dipole moments. Q-Chem Manual
  • Implemented and optimized core numerical components for MRSF-TDDFT calculations, including the modification of iterative Davidson diagonalizer and performance-critical tensor contractions.
  • Implemented density-matrix-based analyses for MRSF-TDDFT using libwfa, including single-excitation character, the effective number of unpaired electrons, and exciton descriptors.
  • Implemented spin–orbit couplings within MRSF-TDDFT using the Wigner–Eckart theorem and a mean-field treatment of the two-electron Breit–Pauli operator.
  • Published the implementation and benchmarks for diradicals, butadiene, and conical intersections in a reduced retinal model (PSB3): J. Chem. Phys. 165, 104101 (2026).
  • Currently developing advanced capabilities, including analytic energy gradients, non-adiabatic couplings, and core–valence separation for MRSF-TDDFT in Q-Chem.

I develop excited-state methods and implement them in Q-Chem, with attention to numerical accuracy and computational cost.
